# Scholarship and Financial Aid Notice, Academic Year 2026-27

## Merit Scholarship

Students ranking in the top 5% of their department by CGPA at the end of the previous academic year are eligible for the Merit Scholarship. The Merit Scholarship covers 50% of tuition fees for the following academic year. A minimum CGPA of 9.0 is required regardless of departmental rank. Merit Scholarships are awarded automatically based on academic records; no separate application is required, but eligible students must confirm acceptance on the student portal by September 30, 2026.

## Need-Based Financial Aid

Students whose family annual income is below Rs. 3,00,000 are eligible to apply for Need-Based Financial Aid. This aid covers up to 75% of tuition fees and, where applicable, hostel fees. Applications require an income certificate issued by a competent government authority, the previous year's income tax returns (if applicable), and a recommendation from the Head of Department. Applications must be submitted through the Financial Aid section of the student portal by September 30, 2026.

## Sports Scholarship

Students who have represented the state or the country in recognized sporting events at the state, national, or international level are eligible for a Sports Scholarship of Rs. 50,000 per year. Applications must include certificates from the relevant sports federation and are reviewed by the Directorate of Sports.

## General Application Instructions

All scholarship and financial aid applications for Academic Year 2026-27 must be submitted online through the student portal under "Scholarships & Financial Aid" no later than September 30, 2026. Late applications will not be considered except in documented medical emergencies, which must be reported to the Dean of Student Affairs within 7 days of the deadline.

Students may hold only one of the Merit Scholarship or Need-Based Financial Aid in a given academic year; the Sports Scholarship may be availed in addition to either.

For queries, contact the Financial Aid Office at financialaid@college.example.edu.
