# Academic Calendar 2026-27

## Odd Semester 2026

Classes for the Odd Semester begin on August 3, 2026. Registration and course add/drop closes on August 10, 2026.

Mid-Semester Examinations are scheduled from September 21, 2026 to September 26, 2026.

Diwali Break runs from October 19, 2026 to October 23, 2026. Classes resume on October 24, 2026.

End-Semester Examinations are scheduled from December 7, 2026 to December 19, 2026.

Winter Break begins December 20, 2026 and continues through January 4, 2027.

## Even Semester 2027

Classes for the Even Semester begin on January 5, 2027. Registration and course add/drop closes on January 12, 2027.

Mid-Semester Examinations are scheduled from February 22, 2027 to February 27, 2027.

Holi Break runs from March 13, 2027 to March 15, 2027.

End-Semester Examinations are scheduled from May 4, 2027 to May 16, 2027.

Summer Break begins May 17, 2027 and continues through July 31, 2027.

## Convocation

The Annual Convocation for graduating students is tentatively scheduled for the first week of August 2027. The exact date will be notified separately by the Academic Section.

## Important Notes

Students should note that the dates for mid-semester and end-semester examinations listed here are institute-wide blocks. Department-specific exam timetables (subject-wise dates, rooms, and seating) are published separately by the Examination Cell at least two weeks before each examination period.

Any changes to this calendar due to unforeseen circumstances (public holidays declared by the state government, campus closures, etc.) will be communicated via official circulars on the student portal.
