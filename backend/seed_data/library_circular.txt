CENTRAL LIBRARY CIRCULAR: BORROWING RULES AND SERVICES

Borrowing Limits

Undergraduate students may borrow up to 4 books at a time for a period of 14 days. Postgraduate students may borrow up to 6 books at a time for a period of 21 days. Faculty members may borrow up to 10 books at a time for a period of 60 days.

Renewal

Books may be renewed once for the same duration as the original loan period, provided no other user has placed a hold on the title. Renewals can be done online through the Library Management System or in person at the circulation desk.

Fines

A fine of Rs. 2 per day per book applies to overdue returns. Fines above Rs. 500 will result in suspension of borrowing privileges until cleared.

Reference Section

Books in the Reference Section, including encyclopedias, dictionaries, back issues of journals, and theses, are for in-library use only and may not be borrowed.

Digital Library Access

All currently enrolled students and faculty have access to the Digital Library, including subscribed e-journal databases and e-books, via the institute network or through VPN when off-campus. Login credentials are the same as the student portal credentials.

Inter-Library Loan

Books not available in the Central Library collection may be requested via Inter-Library Loan (ILL) from partner institutions. ILL requests are submitted through the library website and typically take 5 to 10 working days to fulfill. A processing fee of Rs. 100 per request applies.

Lost or Damaged Books

Users must pay the replacement cost of the book plus a processing fee of Rs. 200 for any book lost or damaged beyond normal wear.

Library Hours

The library is open from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM on weekdays and 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekends, except during examination periods when hours are extended until midnight.
