PLACEMENT CELL GUIDELINES, 2026-27 PLACEMENT SEASON

Eligibility Criteria

Students are eligible to register with the Placement Cell if they have a CGPA of 6.0 or above at the time of registration and have no active backlog (standing arrear) in any course. Students with a backlog that is cleared before the placement drive date may petition the Placement Cell for eligibility on a case-by-case basis, supported by updated grade records from the Academic Section.

Registration Process

All final-year students must register on the Placement Portal at the start of the placement season. Registration requires an updated resume in the prescribed format, a passport-size photograph, and consent for the One Offer Policy described below.

One Offer Policy

Under the One Offer Policy, once a student accepts a job offer through campus placements, they are not permitted to sit for further placement drives, except for companies classified as "Dream Companies" (average package above Rs. 20,00,000 per annum), for which students may participate even after accepting an earlier offer, subject to Placement Cell approval.

Dress Code

Formal business attire is mandatory for all placement-related interactions, including pre-placement talks, group discussions, and interviews.

Pre-Placement Talks

Companies conduct Pre-Placement Talks (PPTs) to describe the role, compensation, and selection process before the selection process begins. Attendance at the PPT is mandatory for any student wishing to sit for that company's selection process, unless the student has informed the Placement Cell in advance of an unavoidable academic conflict.

Selection Process

The typical selection process includes an online aptitude test, followed by technical interviews and, for some companies, a group discussion round and a final HR interview. Exact rounds vary by recruiter and are communicated in advance.

Code of Conduct

Students found to have shared confidential interview questions, engaged in malpractice during online tests, or misrepresented their academic record to a recruiter will be barred from placements for the remainder of the season and referred to the Disciplinary Committee.
